# Magba sufficiency for PCB motor physics

## What does the pinned library actually solve?

### Takeaway

Keep magba as the analytical prescribed-source B-field engine. Version 0.7.2 is not a nonlinear material, eddy-current, or self-consistent magnetization solver, and changing analytical libraries alone would not fix the current backplane errors. [Source API](https://docs.rs/magba/0.7.2/magba/base/trait.Source.html), [local backplane limits](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/backplane/mod.rs#L24-L99)

### Cited Findings

- The workspace pins `magba = "=0.7.2"`; the simulation crate inherits that dependency. The cached package identifies the exact upstream as `https://github.com/p-sira/magba`, author Sira Pornsiriprasert, BSD-3-Clause. Its `.cargo_vcs_info.json` records commit `8a3628eb52ebfeb271caadb1c87d5d495446d05b`. This assessment concerns that release, not a potentially different latest version. [Workspace manifest](../../Cargo.toml#L39), [simulation manifest](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/Cargo.toml#L9-L15), [versioned upstream manifest](https://docs.rs/crate/magba/0.7.2/source/Cargo.toml), [exact upstream commit](https://github.com/p-sira/magba/tree/8a3628eb52ebfeb271caadb1c87d5d495446d05b)
- The license permits source/binary redistribution and modification with retained copyright, conditions and disclaimer, and forbids using the copyright holder/contributor names for endorsement without permission. There is no copyleft requirement in this license. [Pinned upstream license](https://github.com/p-sira/magba/blob/8a3628eb52ebfeb271caadb1c87d5d495446d05b/LICENSE)
- Available magnet sources are cuboid, cylinder, sphere, tetrahedron, triangle, dipole and triangular `MeshMagnet`. The mesh is a prescribed-polarization boundary geometry, not a finite-element material mesh. Its constructor takes one polarization vector and requires a closed, connected, outward-oriented, non-self-intersecting mesh for correct field computation. [Magnet module](https://docs.rs/magba/0.7.2/magba/magnets/index.html), [mesh implementation](https://github.com/p-sira/magba/blob/8a3628eb52ebfeb271caadb1c87d5d495446d05b/src/magnets/mesh.rs#L15-L50)
- Available current sources are `CircularCurrent`, `PathCurrent`, `TriangleCurrent` and `SheetCurrent`. `TriangleCurrent` takes a homogeneous surface current-density vector in A/m; these are specified sources, not computed conduction currents. [Current module](https://docs.rs/magba/0.7.2/magba/currents/index.html), [triangle implementation](https://github.com/p-sira/magba/blob/8a3628eb52ebfeb271caadb1c87d5d495446d05b/src/currents/triangle.rs#L10-L38)
- Public `Source<T: RealField>` exposes `compute_B` and batched B evaluation. Magnets accept polarization in tesla, pose and dimensions; a cuboid has no permeability, conductivity or B-H-curve parameter. The published API/source has no force/torque solver, nonlinear B-H iteration, soft-material coupling, induced-current solve, or native complex-frequency field interface. These absences were checked in the cached release's source modules and public API, not inferred merely from README wording. [Source trait](https://github.com/p-sira/magba/blob/8a3628eb52ebfeb271caadb1c87d5d495446d05b/src/base/source.rs#L16-L49), [cuboid definition](https://github.com/p-sira/magba/blob/8a3628eb52ebfeb271caadb1c87d5d495446d05b/src/magnets/cuboid.rs#L10-L43), [complete public items](https://docs.rs/magba/0.7.2/magba/all.html)
- `mag_to_pol` implements J = mu0 M. `B_to_H` simply divides B by mu0. That helper is the vacuum conversion, not an internal-material constitutive solve: inside a magnet H = B/mu0 - M. Do not mistake its existence for nonlinear H-field/material support. [Conversion implementation](https://github.com/p-sira/magba/blob/8a3628eb52ebfeb271caadb1c87d5d495446d05b/src/conversion.rs#L12-L59)
- Magba explicitly acknowledges that most field computation is based on Magpylib; its README says tests compare against Magpylib and reference data. Agreement with Magpylib therefore verifies consistency of related analytical implementations, not independent validation of soft-magnetic material physics. [Versioned documentation](https://docs.rs/magba/0.7.2/magba/#acknowledgment), [upstream README](https://github.com/p-sira/magba/blob/8a3628eb52ebfeb271caadb1c87d5d495446d05b/README.md#testing)

### Inferences

- The right physical interpretation is fixed, homogeneous polarization within each supplied magnet and linear superposition of prescribed sources in free space. Magnetization does not change in response to neighboring magnets, coils, a yoke, or a housing. A finite magnet's geometric self-demagnetizing field can be represented by its analytic field; the material's response to that field, recoil permeability and irreversible demagnetization are not solved. [Cuboid source parameters](https://github.com/p-sira/magba/blob/8a3628eb52ebfeb271caadb1c87d5d495446d05b/src/magnets/cuboid.rs#L10-L43), [Source API](https://docs.rs/magba/0.7.2/magba/base/trait.Source.html)
- An external iterative magnetization or BEM-style solver could use magba as a field kernel, but that iteration, discretization, constitutive law, convergence and force validation would be new solver work. `MeshMagnet` alone does not provide it. Likewise, separately evaluating sine/cosine source amplitudes can synthesize an imposed AC field, but does not solve skin effect, phase-lagged induced currents or complex permeability. [Mesh parameters](https://github.com/p-sira/magba/blob/8a3628eb52ebfeb271caadb1c87d5d495446d05b/src/magnets/mesh.rs#L15-L50), [real-valued Source interface](https://docs.rs/magba/0.7.2/magba/base/trait.Source.html)

### Gaps

- No independent full-motor FEM or experimental validation of the bare analytical motor thrust was established in this review. Local Magpylib checks concern bare/image-magnet fields and static image attraction, not a self-consistent three-dimensional material solve. [Local comparison scope](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L239-L277)

## Where do present motor predictions and validation come from?

### Takeaway

Magba supplies only B samples. Native Rust code integrates thrust, and a separate native backplane model supplies approximate saturation, complex AC reflection, attraction and drag. FEMM validates restricted 2-D cases independently, while the thick-plate saturation and real 3-D drag limits remain unresolved. [Physics adapter](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/physics/mod.rs), [FEMM results](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L279-L519)

### Cited Findings

- `src/physics/mod.rs` wraps `CuboidMagnet`, `SourceAssembly` and point-parallel `compute_B`. The actual implementation contains no PathCurrent wrapper despite the older mapping table mentioning it. The application uses native F = I sum(dL cross B) quadrature and reverses stator force to report mover force; native code also sums torque. Magba is not computing motor thrust directly. [Adapter lines 7-78](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/physics/mod.rs#L7-L78), [force implementation lines 476-503](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/magnetic/force_eval/mod.rs#L476-L503), [force documentation](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/docs/API.md#L285-L300)
- Backplane images are constructed by mirroring the real cuboids across the PCB bottom and multiplying their polarization by the stack's R. This is exact in the thick infinite mu-to-infinity plane limit; otherwise the fundamental's R is reused for all harmonics. The soft-magnetic response comes from application code, not from magba detecting material near a magnet. [Image construction](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/magnetic/magnet_model/arrangements/backplane.rs#L1-L28)
- The five latest commits inspected were `8201647` saturation/eddy model, `625c0df` drive integration, `8ce8974` design-panel outputs, `d76ce98` thick-layer/bias refinements and `85aacae` FEMM checks. The resulting documented model uses clipped two-slope J(H), describing-function effective permeability solved per thickness sublayer, directional incremental permeability averaged over a pole pitch, and linear harmonic layered reflection with gamma = sqrt(kappa² + j omega mu0 mu sigma). [Current equations and limitations](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/backplane/mod.rs#L7-L99), [recent FEMM section](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L290-L325)
- Static saturated R feeds image magnets and K_f; the AC coil factor gives L = L0 Re F and delta-R = -omega L0 Im F. Traveling-magnet drag uses omega = k v, with force proportional to -Im R and power F v. The motion model retains DC effective permeability, and K_f still uses static R. These are separate approximations, not one coupled transient solution. [Backplane module equations](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/backplane/mod.rs#L48-L92)
- Local Magpylib 5.2.3 checks report magba/Magpylib Bz agreement to 2e-16 T for the tested cuboids and scaled images. For five 5-mm-deep magnets, the application's spectral static attraction is 3.67 N versus 3.89 N from image-force integration, -5.6%; the uncorrected 2-D expression is 5.63 N. This tests the imposed image approximation and finite-depth integration, not actual nonlinear material response. [Local analytical comparison](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L239-L266)
- FEMM 4.2 is an independent 2-D finite-element method here, using one periodic electrical period, a distant A = 0 boundary, nonlinear B-H points, weighted-stress forces, time-harmonic sources and incremental permeability on a prior nonlinear bias solution. No constant was fitted to FEMM. The harness is reproducible under Wine, but this review read the recorded results rather than rerunning it. [Setup](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L1-L50), [nonlinear/AC setup](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L356-L380), [harness description](../../scripts/femm/README.md#L56-L62)
- Thin saturated ferrite sheets at 0.1, 0.2 and 1 mm pass the recorded benchmark tolerances: field ratio within 1.4%, attraction about +6 to +7%, mean sheet flux +6 to +8%. Thick near-knee plates do not: 5 mm flexible ferrite overpredicts attraction by 51.9% and field ratio by 12.9%; 2 mm MnZn attraction is +13.5%. The same failure survives using the model's two-slope B-H curve in FEMM, so it is not fixed by matching knee shape. [Nonlinear tables](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L382-L413), [interpretation](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L453-L471)
- Recorded cause of thick-plate errors is missing normal-field saturation at the face, while the current iteration only saturates from in-plane field. Peak B errors reach +34% for 5 mm flexible ferrite and about +15-16% for MnZn. Suggested safe thicknesses are lower bounds, not established design solutions. Saturating J does not cap total B at Bsat: B = mu0 H + J can exceed that value. [Discussion](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L461-L471), [module clarification](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/backplane/mod.rs#L41-L56)
- Incremental inductance is within 3% of the mean of two tested coil positions, but FEMM ranges from 1.107 to 1.588 for 2 mm MnZn depending on position. The scalar model suppresses this saliency. Its sheet average also omits interlayer mutual coupling and uses evenly spaced copper layers. [Incremental table](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L415-L421), [limits](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L492-L515)
- The 100 kHz linear harmonic coil tests agree to 0.5% in Re F and 1.3% in added resistance. Traveling-wave 2-D drag agrees within 2.1% over the tested 1-100 m/s cases; normal force is within 3.6% above the numerical floor. These tests use fixed material permeability/conductivity, including the model's chosen effective values for saturated-stack cases. They do not independently validate nonlinear dynamic ferrite response. [AC tables](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L423-L451), [discussion](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L478-L490)
- Explicitly unverified are the real 5-mm-depth magnets' 3-D drag, travel-direction array-end entry/exit eddies, magnet-coverage weighting and conductivity at operating temperature. Hysteresis and core loss are absent. A scratch 3-D image-inductance estimate gives 1.523 versus model 1.593 and FEMM slice 1.630, illustrating finite-length effects even without nonlinear materials. [3-D inductance check](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L268-L277), [unverified effects](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L485-L517)
- The version-2 estimate of 0.86 N drag at 20 m/s versus 0.0715 N thrust is a serious screening warning, not a validated 3-D operating prediction. Its apparent precision exceeds the demonstrated validation scope. Attraction is not yet applied to the bearing normal load in the friction budget. [Version-2 table](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L327-L353), [remaining integration gap](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L518-L519)

### Inferences

- The current major error is in the application material approximation, not evidence of a defect in magba's cuboid B formula. Replacing magba with Magpylib would preserve the same prescribed-magnet limitation and would not remove the +14-52% attraction errors. [Related analytical origins](https://docs.rs/magba/0.7.2/magba/#acknowledgment), [same-curve FEMM comparison](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L404-L413)
- Simple reversal of coil Lorentz force is appropriate for the direct prescribed magnet-coil interaction used by the fast model. A future coupled model with a stationary magnetic/conducting backplane must identify forces on each body separately; global reaction balances do not guarantee that all stator-coil reaction belongs to the mover when another body exchanges force. Use mover stress/virtual-work force for the authoritative coupled solution, and verify agreement with coil integration in a suitable limiting case. [Existing force convention](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/docs/API.md#L292-L300), [separate stack/magnet stress outputs in FEMM](../../scripts/femm/README.md#L60)

### Gaps

- The recorded FEMM mesh-convergence check predates the latest layered-sheet/nonlinear cases. The results explicitly say it was not repeated for the layered sheets. Do not describe every new nonlinear and AC case as mesh-converged without further work. [Mesh-check scope](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L45-L50)
- No measured B-H/hysteresis/loss data for the actual cracked flexible sheet was established. Local Bsat defaults use bulk NiZn analogues, while the cited sheet's nominal total thickness includes nonmagnetic layers. [Material assumptions](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L315-L325)

## Should magba be kept, replaced or augmented?

### Takeaway

Keep magba behind the existing physics adapter and augment the application with an independent material-aware field solver path. Replace or correct the backplane approximation where its known error exceeds the design tolerance; do not replace the verified analytical source engine merely to acquire capabilities it was never intended to provide. [Existing adapter boundary](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/physics/mod.rs#L1-L5), [demonstrated error envelope](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L453-L517)

### Cited Findings

- There is already a narrow adapter isolating magba, and the backplane model is already separate from it. This is a suitable starting boundary for preserving fast pure-Rust interactive sweeps while adding a higher-fidelity backend. [Adapter](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/physics/mod.rs), [module map](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/docs/API.md#L33-L46)
- Existing FEMM support already accepts nonlinear B-H curves, prior-solution incremental permeability, complex sources and separate force/loss outputs, but is offline and 2-D. It is an immediately useful reference backend, not proof that the current real 3-D motor has been solved. [FEMM harness](../../scripts/femm/README.md#L1-L18), [supported case outputs](../../scripts/femm/README.md#L56-L62)

### Inferences

- Recommended analytical layer: magba owns prescribed permanent-magnet/current geometry and B evaluation only. Preserve its version pin and cuboid/pose/unit regression tests. Native routed-copper quadrature owns fast Lorentz thrust. The application material model owns planar reflections and should report its assumptions and applicability separately. This follows the existing code boundary and validation split. [Adapter](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/physics/mod.rs), [native force integration](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/magnetic/force_eval/mod.rs#L476-L503), [backplane limits](../../crates/pcbmotorgen-simulation/src/backplane/mod.rs#L94-L99)
- Recommended reference/accurate layer: solve the nonlinear magnetostatic bias with vector H and measured B-H data, then solve tensor incremental harmonic response about that state. Return B samples, flux linkage or the winding inductance matrix, complex impedance, mover/stack force separately, Joule loss and convergence evidence. Full self-consistency must include induced soft-material magnetization; include permanent-magnet recoil permeability and demagnetization only when required by the operating field envelope. This addresses the missing normal-field saturation, position-dependent incremental response and omitted interlayer coupling observed locally. [Diagnosed saturation failure](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L461-L475), [interlayer-coupling limitation](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L492-L501), [magba's fixed source model](https://docs.rs/magba/0.7.2/magba/magnets/struct.CuboidMagnet.html)
- Keep the layered harmonic eddy model as a fast estimate for infinite planar, constant-property stacks. Escalate finite housing, short magnet depth, array ends, slots/cuts and coupled motion/material nonlinearities to 3-D magnetoquasistatic analysis. Validate force against Joule-loss power F v, finite-domain and mesh convergence, and geometry sweeps before treating the present 3-D drag numbers as design constraints. [Successful 2-D checks and power check](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L437-L451), [unverified 3-D effects](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L485-L490)
- Short-term priority is a material-solver correction or explicit out-of-envelope warning for thick near-knee plates, not another scalar permeability fit. An anisotropic per-sublayer extension may help, but it remains a reduced model until it passes the current failed FEMM cases and independent normal/in-plane field tests. Next validate actual routed geometry and finite-width drag in 3-D. [Failure explanation](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L461-L471), [3-D geometry sensitivity](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L268-L277)
- Validation labels should distinguish analytical equivalence with Magpylib, independent 2-D FEM verification for a stated constitutive model, independent 3-D verification, and measured hardware validation. Preserve input geometry, material curve provenance, bias state, frequency/speed, mesh tolerances and force convention with cached results. These labels prevent the present 2-D ~2% eddy agreement from being read as validation of untested 3-D or hysteretic behavior. [Different comparison scopes](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L239-L277), [FEMM setup](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L356-L380), [remaining physics limits](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L485-L517)

### Gaps

- This assessment does not choose a specific replacement FEM package or establish its licensing, Rust integration, performance or nonlinear moving-conductor support. Those require a separate candidate evaluation. FEMM is the verified existing reference path, not an endorsed 3-D production solution. [Existing FEMM scope](../../scripts/femm/README.md#L1-L5)
- No code or simulation runs were changed. Evidence is the pinned cached upstream source, official release documentation and the checkout's recorded results as read on 2026-10-18. Untracked user assets were left untouched. [Pinned release](https://docs.rs/magba/0.7.2/magba/), [recorded run date](../../scripts/femm/RESULTS.md#L1-L9)
